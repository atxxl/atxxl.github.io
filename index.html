Sarcasm Detection with Transformer Architectures NLPXAI

Theses

Interpretable Sentiment Analysis with Concept Bottleneck Models

MSc thesis · DTU · 2024
  • Studied how sparsity trades off interpretability against accuracy in Concept Bottleneck Models on GoEmotions (28 emotion concepts, heavy class imbalance). Reproduced Sparse CBMs as a starting point; ran 9 experiment groups / 73 tracked runs on W&B with data augmentation and hyperparameter tuning, reaching a best accuracy of 80% (std. err. as low as 0.043).
  • Compared models with confusion matrices and tested significance with McNemar's test; showed that tuned sparsity can preserve accuracy while improving interpretability.

Approach for Designing Moral Machine

BSc thesis · University of Copenhagen · 2019
  • Compared top-down (rule-based) and bottom-up (learned from data) approaches to building a machine that makes moral choices in trolley-problem dilemmas.
  • For the bottom-up part, trained decision trees in Python on the public Moral Machine dataset (MIT Media Lab; 38 million rows): a depth-4 tree reached 69% accuracy and largely matched the global preferences reported in the original study; an unconstrained tree reached 73%. Chose a white-box model deliberately and argued that algorithms with large social impact should stay comprehensible.
